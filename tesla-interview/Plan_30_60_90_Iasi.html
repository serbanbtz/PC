30-60-90 Day Plan – Tesla Iași · Sales Supervisor

Three principles

  1. Learn Tesla's way first, then improve it. I don't come in to change the process. I come in to execute it perfectly in a new market.
  2. Customers and deliveries first. Every decision is tested against one question: does it make the customer's experience better?
  3. Build a team that runs without me. Success at day 90 is a store that performs on the days I'm not there.

Days 1–30 · Learn and set the foundations

Area What I do
Tesla Complete onboarding and product training. Spend time at another Romanian store (Bucharest or Timișoara) to see deliveries, the daily routine and a quarter-end up close.
Team A 1:1 with every team member: strengths, goals, what they need from me. If hiring is still open, join the interviews and hire for attitude and coachability.
Service Meet the Service and Parts team in Brătuleni. Agree on one clear escalation path and on how a car moves from Sales to Service.
Market Build a map of Iași: existing Tesla owners in the region, the top [20] employers (IT, medical, university), leasing companies, the local events calendar.
Routines Start the 10-minute morning huddle and a daily funnel check from day one.

By day 30: every team member has a development goal. The market map and the launch priorities are agreed with my manager.

Days 31–60 · Launch and build demand

Area What I do
Test drives Weekend test-drive events, test-drive days at large employers ([company 1, company 2]), invitations for existing owners and the referral program.
Financing A short, simple guide for advisors and customers: Rabla 2026 (18,500 lei), private leasing, company purchase and company leasing. That's where many buyers here get stuck.
Deliveries A readiness check 3 days before every delivery: payment, financing, documents, trade-in. No surprises on delivery day.
Coaching On the floor during peak hours. I join customer conversations, then debrief for five minutes. Weekly 1:1s on numbers and development.

By day 60: a weekly test-drive rhythm is running and the first deliveries are completed with no open issues at handover.

Days 61–90 · Optimise and scale

Area What I do
Funnel Compare our funnel with the other Romanian stores and fix the single biggest bottleneck.
Quarter-end Plan staffing and delivery waves weeks ahead. Pre-delivery readiness becomes the main focus in the last month of the quarter.
People Identify the future leaders and give them ownership: a financing champion, a delivery champion.
Customer feedback With Service: the 5 questions customers ask most after delivery get answered at delivery.

By day 90: a team that runs the daily routine on its own, a clear plan for the first quarter-end, and at least one person on track to grow into a bigger role.

What I watch every day (the funnel)

Stage What I look at Why
Opportunities New leads, walk-ins, event contacts Is awareness working in a new market?
Test drives Number per week, per advisor The strongest sales tool Tesla has
Orders Test drive → order conversion Where coaching has the biggest effect
Readiness % of deliveries ready 3 days before Protects deliveries already sold
Deliveries On time, no open issues at handover The moment customers remember
Satisfaction Customer feedback after delivery Next to conversion, never instead of it

Risks I already see, and how I'd handle them

Risk What I'd do
Tesla is new in the Moldova region Test drives everywhere, local owners as ambassadors, presence at local events
MG and BYD compete hard on price (MG4 was the top EV in Romania in August) Win on what the store controls: test drives, service close to home in Brătuleni, the delivery experience, total cost of ownership
Rabla dropped from 37,000 to 18,500 lei Financing and leasing explained simply, especially for companies
A brand-new team Structured onboarding, shadowing deliveries, a 30-day check-in for every new hire

The 60-second spoken version

(Folosește-o în locul răspunsului 5E din ghid.)

In the first 30 days I'd learn: Tesla's processes, time in another Romanian store, a one-on-one with every team member, and a map of the local market: owners, big employers, events. And I'd meet the Service team in Brătuleni early, because Sales and Service need to work as one store.

Days 30 to 60 are about launching demand: test-drive events, company test-drive days, a simple financing guide (Rabla, leasing, company purchase) and a readiness check three days before every delivery.

Days 60 to 90 I'd optimise: compare our funnel with the other Romanian stores, fix the biggest bottleneck, plan the first quarter-end, and find the people who'll grow into leaders.

Success at day 90 is a store that runs well on the days I'm not there.

Pe scurt în română

  • Zilele 1–30, învăț: procesele Tesla, timp într-un alt magazin din România, 1:1 cu fiecare om din echipă, întâlnire cu Service-ul din Brătuleni și harta pieței din Iași (proprietari Tesla, angajatori mari, evenimente).
  • Zilele 31–60, lansez: evenimente de test drive, zile de test drive la firme, ghid simplu de finanțare (Rabla, leasing, firme), verificare cu 3 zile înainte de fiecare livrare.
  • Zilele 61–90, optimizez: compar pâlnia cu celelalte magazine, rezolv cel mai mare blocaj, planific primul final de trimestru și identific viitorii lideri.
  • Succesul la 90 de zile: un magazin care merge bine și în zilele în care eu nu sunt acolo.
